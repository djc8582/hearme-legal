# Privacy Policy — HearMe

**Last updated: 22 September 2026**

HearMe is a music creation app. You record or play a short musical idea, add
some words, and the app builds a finished song around them. This policy
explains what the app collects, where it goes, and how to get rid of it.

We do not sell your data, we do not show advertising, and the app contains no
analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software of any kind.

## What we collect

**Account information.** When you create an account we store your email
address, a display name, a public handle other users can search for, and an
optional profile photo. Your password is handled by our authentication provider
and is stored only as a salted hash — it is never visible to us.

**The music you make.** Recordings you make in the app, audio files you upload,
melodies you play on the keyboard or a connected MIDI device, lyrics you write
or generate, and the finished songs the app produces. These are stored in your
account so your projects and library are there next time you open the app.

**Social information, if you use the community features.** Friend requests and
friendships, songs you publish to your friends, and likes and play counts on
those songs. Publishing is always something you choose to do — songs are private
to you until you share them.

**Notification token.** If you allow notifications, we store the push token that
identifies your device so we can tell you about things like incoming friend
requests. It is not linked to any advertising identifier.

## Device permissions

- **Microphone** — to record singing, humming, or an instrument. Only while you
  are actively recording.
- **Camera** and **Photo Library** — only if you choose to set a profile picture.

The app does not request or collect location, contacts, health data, or your
device's advertising identifier.

## Where your data goes

**Storage.** Your account, projects, audio and social data are stored using
Appwrite Cloud, hosted in the United States.

**Music generation and analysis.** To create a song, the app uploads the audio
you provide, along with your lyrics, to a research server operated by the
Georgia Institute of Technology Center for Music Technology
(`cmt.music.gatech.edu`). Finished songs are also uploaded there for musical
analysis — the app uses this to work out the song's structure and harmony so it
can draw the visualisation you see on the result screen. This processing is what
makes the app work; without it no song can be generated.

**Lyric processing.** Lyrics are also processed by Alibaba Cloud's DashScope
API (dashscope-intl.aliyuncs.com), which formats them for the music model. In
the web app, word suggestions in the lyric editor are looked up via Datamuse
(api.datamuse.com). Neither is used for advertising.

Other than the services named above, which process data only to make the app
work, we do not share your data with anyone, and we do not use it for
advertising.

## How long we keep it

Your content stays in your account until you delete it or delete your account.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your entire account from inside the app: **Profile → Delete
Account**. This permanently removes your songs and projects, your uploaded and
recorded audio, your friendships, anything you have published, likes and plays
you have left on other people's songs, your profile, and finally your login
itself. It cannot be undone.

To delete an individual song or library item, remove it from within the app.

If you would rather we did it for you, email the address below and we will
action it.

## Children

HearMe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us
and we will remove it.

## User content and conduct

Songs published to friends are user-generated content. We do not tolerate
unlawful or abusive material. If you encounter something objectionable, contact
us at the address below and we will review and remove it.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top of this
page.
