# HearMe — Support

Need a hand? Email **cantor.dylan@gmail.com** and we will get back to you.

## What HearMe does

Record or hum a musical idea, play a melody on the built-in keyboard, or upload
something you already have. Add a few lines of lyrics — or let the app draft
them. HearMe builds the rest of the song around your idea: drums, bass, harmony
and vocals. When it's done you can listen to it and explore it as an interactive
visualisation.

## Common questions

**How long does a song take to generate?**
Usually two to five minutes. Songs are generated on a university research
server, so the wait depends on how busy it is. You'll see your place in the
queue while you wait.

**What do I need before I can generate?**
At least one musical input — a recording, an uploaded file, or a melody you
played — and some lyrics. The app will tell you which piece is still missing.

**Nothing happens when I tap Generate.**
Check your internet connection. Generation needs to reach our server, so it does
not work offline.

**Can I use HearMe without an account?**
You can record, play melodies and write lyrics as a guest. You'll be asked to
sign up when you generate your first song, so that we have somewhere to keep it.

**My recording sounds quiet or clipped.**
Hold the phone about a hand's width from your mouth, and avoid covering the
microphone at the bottom edge. It does not need to be a perfect take — the app
is listening for the idea, not the performance.

**How do I share a song with a friend?**
Add them under Friends, then share a song from your library. You can share it
listen-only, or let them open your inputs and build their own version.

**How do I delete my account?**
Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your songs, audio,
friendships and profile. It cannot be undone.

**How do I report something objectionable?**
Email **cantor.dylan@gmail.com** with details of the song or user and we will
review it and take it down if appropriate.
